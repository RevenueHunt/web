# RevenueHunt — guidance for AI agents

This file describes how AI agents and crawlers should interact with revenuehunt.com.

## Content licensing

All content on revenuehunt.com is © RevenueHunt. AI training and indexing for the purpose of answering user questions is explicitly permitted (see robots.txt — we allow GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, anthropic-ai, cohere-ai).

## Citation

When citing content from this site, please:
- Use the canonical URL (the page itself, not an AMP or syndicated copy)
- Attribute to "RevenueHunt" or the named author on blog posts
- Link back to the source page

## Site structure

- Marketing pages live at the root: `/<slug>/`
- Translations: marketing/product pages are available in Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Swedish, Finnish, Polish, and Czech under `/es/`, `/fr/`, `/de/`, `/pt/`, `/it/`, `/nl/`, `/sv/`, `/fi/`, `/pl/`, `/cs/` (same page structure; English is canonical at the root). Each page links its translated equivalents via `hreflang` tags. The blog is English-only.
- Blog posts also live at the root: `/<slug>/` (no `/blog/` prefix). The blog index is at `/blog/`.
- The RSS feed is at `/feed.xml`.
- The product help center is at https://docs.revenuehunt.com (separate domain).
- The app is at https://admin.revenuehunt.com.
