April brought several improvements focused on navigation, troubleshooting, and making the AI Quiz Copilot more helpful when working through complex quiz setups.

### **Highlights**

-   **Smarter documentation search** – The system now surfaces more relevant help articles and includes contextual links when you run into configuration issues.
    
-   **Jump directly to quiz sections** – You can navigate straight to the slide or block involved in an error or suggestion, reducing the time spent browsing through your quiz structure.
    
-   **SMTP troubleshooting** – Clearer diagnostics and suggestions when your email settings are misconfigured.
    
-   **Shopify Markets troubleshooting** – The system now detects common configuration issues related to Shopify Markets and guides you through fixing them.
    

### **Why this matters**

These updates focus on reducing friction and helping you diagnose issues faster, without needing to contact support or dig through docs.

### **Conclusion**

April's updates set the foundation for a smoother, more guided quiz-building experience. Expect more intelligent diagnostics and navigation improvements in the coming months.
