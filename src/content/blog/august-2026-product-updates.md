August was about what happens right after a shopper acts: products added from quiz results now show up in your theme's cart drawer immediately, quizzes are quicker to start, saved results open inside inline quiz blocks, and the whole app moved to Shopify's newest APIs.

We shipped cart drawer refresh on quiz add-to-cart, a refreshed Create quiz menu, saved-result links for inline quizzes, Shopify's 2026-07 API version, in-app feedback, shop-specific pricing in Copilot, and sturdier WooCommerce catalogue syncs.

## Highlights

-   **Cart drawers keep up with your quiz.** Products added from quiz results now appear immediately in your theme's cart drawer and cart counter, including Dawn, Sense, and the Horizon family. On stores without a drawer, shoppers stay in the quiz flow instead of being sent off to the full cart page. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/update-shopify-cart-drawer/)
-   **A refreshed Create quiz menu.** The **Create quiz** button on the Quizzes page now gathers every starting point in one place: start with AI, from a blank quiz, from a template, from an imported file, or from a legacy migration when your shop is eligible. [Make your first quiz](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/tutorials/making-first-quiz/) · [Migrate a legacy quiz](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/migrate-shopify-legacy-quiz/)
-   **Saved results now open inside inline quizzes.** Links using `#response-...` or `?response_id=...` load a previously saved response directly in an inline quiz block, not just in a popup. Paired with July's trackable result links, a shopper clicking back from an email lands on their own recommendations, in place, on the page you chose. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/publish-quiz-inline/)
-   **Shopify's newest APIs.** The app and the quiz runtime now run on Shopify's 2026-07 API version, so RevenueHunt keeps pace with the latest Shopify has to offer.
-   **Tell us what you think, from inside the app.** A new Feedback page in the side menu lets you report a bug, request a feature, suggest a new app, or send general thoughts straight to the product team. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/feedback/)
-   **Copilot quotes your actual price.** Ask Copilot what a plan will cost and you'll get your shop's real price, discounts and custom offers included. It also troubleshoots Recharge more precisely when subscription options don't show up in live quiz results. [Use Quiz Copilot](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/use-quiz-copilot/) · [Recommend subscription products](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/recommend-subscription-products/)
-   **Sturdier WooCommerce catalogue syncs.** Temporary WooCommerce API failures are now retried, and your existing products and collections stay in place until a sync completes successfully.
-   **A clearer plans page.** Plans & Pricing now shows your shop's actual plan, and the 30-day money-back guarantee is stated plainly. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/customer-success/plans-pricing-faq/)
-   **A richer Resources page.** The **Resources** page in the side menu collects the tools, reports, and templates that help you plan a quiz: the quiz ROI calculator, our Shopify quizzes benchmark report, the template library, and a directory of apps that pair well with a quiz. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/resources/)
-   **Builder fixes.** Jump and skip logic share one simpler rule editor, the template picker loads properly, and inline quizzes in the legacy app return to your configured height after a retake. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/quiz-builder/conditional-logic/)

## What's coming

-   **Agent-ready commerce.** We're working on making your quiz's personal-shopper logic available beyond your store, so the same tailored guidance can reach customers wherever they start their shopping, including the AI assistants they increasingly turn to. More on this soon.

## Conclusion

August pushed on the moments right around the quiz: the add to cart that finally updates your theme's drawer, the first click that starts a new quiz, the emailed link that reopens a saved result in place. Underneath, the app moved to Shopify's newest APIs, WooCommerce syncs got sturdier, and Copilot learned your real plan price. And with feedback now a page inside the app, telling us what to build next takes about ten seconds.
