Each one below is reviewed with its free-plan limit and the type of recommendation it powers.

## What does a Shopify product recommendation app do?

A Shopify product recommendation app suggests products to a shopper based on signals: stated preferences (quiz answers), behaviour (pages visited, items in cart), context (what other customers bought, time of year), or post-purchase intent (what's typically bought next). Some apps focus on a single signal; the strongest stacks combine several.

The four mechanics that consistently move revenue:

- **Product recommendation quizzes** for higher conversion on cold traffic
- **Personalised email-based product marketing** for retention
- **Upsell and cross-sell apps** for AOV
- **Subscription and bundle builders** for repeat purchase

## How to get the most from Shopify product recommendation apps

A few principles consistently separate stores that get strong ROI from product recommendation apps from stores that install and forget:

- **Match the app to your funnel goal.** Quizzes lift conversion on cold traffic. Upsell apps lift AOV. Post-purchase recommenders lift attached-sale rate. Pick the one that matches your weakest funnel metric, not the one with the loudest pitch.
- **Theme compatibility matters.** Recommendation widgets only convert if they fit the visual rhythm of your store. Minimal, modern product pages tend to make recommendations more readable.
- **Send recommendations to the customer's stated context.** Generic "you might also like" widgets convert worse than recommendations anchored to the customer's quiz answers, purchase history or browsing pattern.
- **Measure the incremental effect.** Most apps report their own "sales" number which over-counts orders that would have happened anyway. Compare baseline AOV and conversion rate before and after install, and gate any paid plan on a real lift.

For the activation side of this (turning quiz answers into segmented Klaviyo flows and Meta Custom Audiences), see [how to make your Facebook ads smarter with quiz audiences](https://revenuehunt.com/make-your-facebook-ads-smarter-with-revenuehunts-quiz-integration/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free product recommendation app for Shopify?

It depends on the funnel goal. For conversion on cold traffic, a recommendation quiz (RevenueHunt) does the most; for AOV, an upsell or post-purchase app (Wiser, ReConvert); for retention, Klaviyo. The genuinely-free options (with a real free tier, not just a trial) are RevenueHunt, Klaviyo, UpPromote, Wiser, ReConvert, PowerBuy, and Product Recommendations PRO.

### Are these apps actually free?

Most have a genuine free tier with usage limits (for example RevenueHunt's 100 quiz completions per month, or ReConvert's 50 orders per month). Two on this list, Amplify and Also Bought, are free-trial-only and then become paid; the at-a-glance table flags which is which so there are no surprises.

### Should I use a quiz or an upsell app?

They solve different problems, and many stores run both. A quiz works at the top of the funnel: it lifts conversion on cold traffic and captures zero-party data you own. Upsell and post-purchase apps work after the shopper is already buying: they lift average order value. Start with whichever matches your weakest funnel metric.

### Do product recommendation apps slow down a Shopify store?

They can if the widgets are heavy or poorly placed, so keep them minimal and theme-matched, and prefer apps that render as native theme blocks. Always measure the incremental lift (compare conversion and AOV before and after install) rather than trusting the app's own attributed-sales number.
