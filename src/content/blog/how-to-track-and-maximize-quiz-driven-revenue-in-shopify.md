### → Track Quiz Revenue with Shopify Revenue Report 📊

RevenueHunt's Shopify Revenue Report feature provides Shopify merchants with a transparent view of how quizzes impact sales. By linking your quiz with Shopify's order tracking system, you can easily monitor customer purchases tied to quiz recommendations.

#### **1️⃣** Enable Shopify Revenue Tracking

1.  **Activate Order Notes** – Make sure Order Notes are enabled in your Shopify Theme Settings. This allows quiz purchase data to be recorded.
2.  **Connect to Shopify Reports** – In the RevenueHunt app, navigate to the **Connect** tab and locate **Shopify Revenue Report (Beta)**. Click 'Connect'.
3.  ![How to Track and Maximize Quiz-Driven Revenue in Shopify - image 2](/img/blog/how-to-track-and-maximize-quiz-driven-revenue-in-shopify/revenuehuntrevenueconnect.webp)
4.  **Grant Access** – Provide the necessary permissions to allow RevenueHunt to access your order data.
5.  **Publish Changes** – Click 'Publish' to finalize the setup and start tracking.

#### **2️⃣** View Your Quiz Revenue Data

Once connected, visit **Metrics > Analytics** in the RevenueHunt app to track:

-   **Number of Orders** – The total number of purchases driven by quiz recommendations.
-   **Total Order Value** – The cumulative revenue from quiz-driven sales.
-   **Average Order Value** – The average amount spent per quiz-generated order.

#### **3️⃣** Maximize Revenue Attribution

For best results, ensure that customers **add products to their cart directly from the quiz results page and proceed to checkout immediately**. Delayed purchases won't be attributed to the quiz, so optimizing your checkout flow is key.

### → Gain Deeper Insights with Google Analytics 📈

While Shopify Revenue Reports show direct quiz-driven sales, integrating your quiz with **Google Analytics** provides a more granular view of customer behavior. This helps you analyze quiz engagement, detect drop-off points, and improve conversions.

#### 1️⃣ Connect Your Quiz to Google Analytics

1.  Navigate to the **Connect** tab in RevenueHunt.
2.  Locate **Google Analytics** and enter your GA4 Tracking Code.
3.  Copy your **Measurement ID** from Google Analytics and paste it into the RevenueHunt app.
4.  Publish the changes to activate tracking.

#### 2️⃣ Monitor Quiz Interactions and Sales

Once set up, Google Analytics tracks:

-   Quiz starts, views, and completion rates.
-   Choices selected and product recommendations.
-   Add-to-cart actions and checkouts.
-   Revenue generated by quiz-driven sales.

Navigate to **Engagement > Conversions > Event Name > Purchase** to view revenue data. Add a **Source** column and look for 'revenuehunt' to isolate quiz-driven purchases.

#### 3️⃣ Optimize Based on Data Insights

Using GA4, you can:

-   Identify high-performing quizzes and optimize lower-performing ones.
-   Track traffic sources leading to quiz engagement.
-   Improve user flow and reduce quiz abandonment rates.
