January focused on making your quiz funnels easier to scale and trust: smoother promos, clearer analytics, more maintainable logic, richer data capture, and a big push on accessibility so more customers can complete your quiz comfortably.  

### Highlights

-   **Order sync status in analytics** – A new header in the analytics view shows whether orders are fully synced and lets you trigger a manual refresh, so you always know how current your revenue data is. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/quiz-builder/metrics/#analytics)
-   **Market & currency-aware carts** – Carts now carry market and currency metadata, and we apply currency conversion for analytics, giving you more accurate revenue and AOV across regions.
-   **Easier logic editing (drag & actions)** – Jump and skip rules can be reordered via drag-and-drop, with new hover actions and menus on logic blocks plus warnings for empty choice blocks, making complex logic much easier to maintain. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/quiz-builder/conditional-logic/)
-   **JS-injected data as “Additional Captured Data”** – Any answers or fields you inject via JavaScript now show up in their own “Additional Captured Data” section in responses, so advanced data stays visible without cluttering core answers. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/add-javascript/)
-   **HubSpot OAuth integration (pending approval)** – Native OAuth 2.0 connection to HubSpot with list selection and quiz-level toggles for lead capture, ready on our side and awaiting HubSpot’s final approval. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-hubspot/)
-   **Smarter Quiz Copilot (voice + validation)** – Copilot now supports voice input in compatible browsers, validates quiz JSON more strictly, and keeps a clearer history of its own tool use, reducing hallucinations and making generated setups more reliable. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/use-quiz-copilot/)
-   **Variant-aware images in result blocks** – Result Product Blocks can include an Image Slot that either shows the default product image or the selected variant image, so visuals match the exact recommendation. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/#slot-item-composition)
-   **Accessibility improvements & guide** – Quizzes are now documented against WCAG 2.1 Level AAA, with keyboard-only navigation, robust screen reader support, high-contrast default themes, large touch targets, motion-respect settings, RTL support, and customizable accessibility text in quiz settings, plus a dedicated guide to help you keep your own customizations compliant. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/accessibility/)
-   **Newsletter promo codes for response boosts** – Redeem special newsletter codes to temporarily increase your monthly response limit, perfect for big campaigns or launches without changing plans. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/app-settings/#newsletter)
