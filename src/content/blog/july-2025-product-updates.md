This month brought major improvements to previews, tracking, and onboarding experience.

### **Highlights**

-   **Display draft products in preview** – Perfect for stores with products not yet published. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/hide-out-of-stock-products/)
    
-   **Optimized image rendering** – Faster load times and better visual quality. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/add-adjust-images/)
    
-   **Improved Meta Pixel tracking** – More reliable event reporting for retargeting. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/integrate-meta-pixel/)
    
-   **Improved GA4 tracking** – Better event consistency across funnels. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/integrate-google-analytics/)
    
-   **Improved quiz preview modal** – Cleaner UI and more accurate behavior simulation.
    
-   **Automatic popup quiz (app block)** – Easily trigger exit-intent or timed popups. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/publish-quiz-automatic-popup/)
    
-   **Onboarding checklist** – New guided steps in the dashboard.
    
-   **Preview draft product recommendations** – Better testing workflows.
    

### **Why this matters**

Between tracking upgrades and improved previews, July was all about giving merchants more accurate data and smoother building workflows.

### **Conclusion**

With stronger tracking and an improved onboarding experience, merchants can now publish with more confidence.
