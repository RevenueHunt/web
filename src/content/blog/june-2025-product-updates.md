June delivered improvements focused on accessibility, inventory-sensitive logic, and better ways to manage complex configurations.

### **Highlights**

-   **Webhook management** – Clearer tools to configure and troubleshoot webhook integrations. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-webhooks/)
    
-   **Dynamic height adjustment for choices** – Better layout behavior for long lists or varying content.
    
-   **Don't recommend low-inventory products** – Automatically prevent sold-out or low-stock items from appearing in recommendations. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/hide-out-of-stock-products/)
    
-   **Improved accessibility** – Keyboard navigation now works throughout the quiz (ADA compliance).
    

### **Why this matters**

Merchants get safer, smarter recommendations and can trust that quiz layouts adapt well across devices while remaining more accessible.

### **Conclusion**

June reinforces stability and usability across both quiz creation and customer experience.
