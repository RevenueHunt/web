June was about reach and reliability: the admin now speaks seven more languages, Shopify list metafields flow into your quizzes, popups are better isolated from one another, and locale handling holds up more dependably across redirects.

We shipped seven new admin languages, more reliable locale handling, support for Shopify list single-line metafields, cleaner popup settings isolation, and a batch of legacy app and internal tooling fixes.

## Highlights

-   **Seven more admin languages.** The RevenueHunt admin is now available in Hebrew, Farsi, Finnish, Czech, Korean, Thai, and Turkish, so more of your team can build and manage quizzes in their own language.
-   **More reliable locale handling.** Language overrides now survive redirects more reliably, and locale files are cache-busted on every deploy, so merchants see updated translations sooner instead of waiting on stale caches.
-   **Support for Shopify list metafields.** Shopify `list.single_line_text_field` metafields can now be imported and rendered, so richer catalogue and product data shows up in your quizzes. Merchants also get clearer guidance about the Storefront API requirements for using metafields. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/add-product-metafields/)
-   **Better popup isolation.** Theme extension popup settings are now better separated, reducing the chance that one popup's configuration affects another.
-   **Legacy app fixes.** The custom JavaScript editor now shows correctly in the legacy app, and it captures Shopify and WooCommerce operator contact information, including contact syncs received from the new app.

## What's coming

-   **Mailchimp integration.** A native Mailchimp connection is on its way, so Mailchimp users will be able to automatically capture quiz leads in their Mailchimp lists. We're putting the finishing touches on it and will let you know as soon as it's ready to connect.

-   **Messaging app integrations (Beta).** Your quiz will soon run as a native conversation inside the channels where your customers already talk to you: **WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger**, TikTok, SMS, and tools like Gorgias or Shopify Inbox. Customers answer questions, get personalized product recommendations with Add to Cart links, and leads get captured in Klaviyo, all without visiting your store. We're already onboarding early beta merchants and would love to hear which channels matter most to you. **[Contact support to request early access →](https://revenuehunt.com/contact/)**

June widened who can use RevenueHunt and made it steadier underneath: seven new admin languages, locale handling that survives redirects, and richer product data from Shopify list metafields. Pair that with cleaner popup isolation and a round of legacy app and tooling fixes, and your quizzes reach more merchants in more languages while staying dependable. With a native Mailchimp connection up next, capturing quiz leads automatically is about to get easier still.
