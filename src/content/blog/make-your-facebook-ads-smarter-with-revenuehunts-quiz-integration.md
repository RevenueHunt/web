🔥 **What products suit their needs?**  
🔥 **What concerns or preferences do they have?**  
🔥 **Which product recommendations do they engage with the most?**

By tracking quiz interactions with the **Facebook Pixel**, you unlock a goldmine of data that can help you **refine your ad campaigns and personalize your customer experience**.

## **How to Integrate Facebook Pixel with Your RevenueHunt Quiz**

Setting up Facebook Pixel with your quiz is **quick and easy**:

1️⃣ **Set Up Your Facebook Pixel**

-   If you haven't already, create a Pixel in **Facebook Business Manager**. Check this [official guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/952192354843755?id=1205376682832142) for detailed instructions.

2️⃣ **Connect Your Facebook Pixel to RevenueHunt**

-   Inside your **RevenueHunt Quiz**, go to the [**Connect**](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/quiz-builder/#connect-integrations) tab.
-   Find the **Facebook Pixel** section and click **Connect**.
-   Enter your **Pixel ID** and save the changes.

![](/img/blog/make-your-facebook-ads-smarter-with-revenuehunts-quiz-integration/Connect-Pixel.png)

3️⃣ **Start Tracking & Optimizing**

-   Once activated, your **Pixel will start firing events** based on user interactions with the quiz.
-   Use **Facebook Events Manager** to view data, analyze user behavior, and adjust your ad strategy accordingly.
