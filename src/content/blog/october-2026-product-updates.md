October was about reach: your quiz supports shoppers navigating by keyboard or screen reader, the admin works comfortably on a phone, and finishing a quiz can now earn loyalty points.

## Highlights

-   **Quizzes that work for every shopper.** Your quiz is straightforward to complete with a keyboard or a screen reader, with progress announced along the way, and popup quizzes keep focus where it belongs. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/accessibility/)
-   **Build and check quizzes from your phone.** The builder, previews, and dashboards all work comfortably at phone and tablet size.
-   **Variant options on your results page.** Each variant's options, such as Size: M or Color: Blue, are now available in results-page product data, ready to show on your recommendation cards. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/)
-   **Reward quiz completions with loyalty points.** You can now award Growave loyalty points when a shopper finishes your quiz, connected through Shopify Flow. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/reward-quiz-completions-with-growave/)
-   **Cart drawers on Eurus, Focal, and Atlas.** Adding a product from quiz results refreshes the drawer and cart count, and the drawer follows your theme's own setting for whether it opens. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/update-shopify-cart-drawer/)
-   **Precise Shopify customer tagging.** Your quiz updates a customer's Shopify tags only when it assigns them, leaving their other tags untouched. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/quiz-builder/customer-tags/)
-   **Omnisend templates with quiz personalisation.** Newly generated Omnisend templates use Omnisend's own tags for quiz answers and recommended products, in an email-friendly layout. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-omnisend/)
-   **Richer Klaviyo events.** The Klaviyo quiz event can carry answers, tags, scores, and up to 10 recommended products, ready to use in flow filters, splits, and email templates. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-klaviyo/)
-   **Inventory filtering follows your setting.** Your inventory filtering setting decides which out-of-stock variants reach your recommendations, at the threshold you choose. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/hide-out-of-stock-products/)

## What's coming

-   **A new version of the Built for Shopify app.** The next version of the [Built for Shopify](/revenuehunt-built-for-shopify/) app is on its way, with more options for designing your quiz and your results page exactly the way you want them.

## Conclusion

October's focus was accessibility: your quiz is now straightforward to complete with a keyboard or a screen reader. Alongside it, the admin works comfortably on a phone, results pages can show variant options, quiz completions can earn loyalty points, and Omnisend and Klaviyo both receive richer quiz data.
