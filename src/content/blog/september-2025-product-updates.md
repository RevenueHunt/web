September delivered powerful UI improvements, better mobile behavior, HTML-enabled templates, and new tools for creating multiple quizzes on the same page.

### **Highlights**

-   **Scoped CSS injection** – You can now safely inject custom CSS that applies only to the quiz, without affecting the rest of your site. This gives more flexibility for advanced customization while maintaining theme stability. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/customize-quiz-design/#advanced-customizations-css)
    
-   **Mobile slider tooltip** – Slider questions now include a clear, tappable tooltip on mobile, making it easier for visitors to understand the selected value and improving overall interaction accuracy.
    
-   **HTML allowed in Klaviyo templates** – Klaviyo templates generated by Quiz Copilot now support basic HTML formatting. This unlocks richer email layouts, better branding, and more engaging follow-ups. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/use-quiz-copilot/)
    
-   **Multiple quizzes on the same page** – You can now embed multiple quizzes on a single page or section. This is especially helpful for multi-product funnels, advanced segmentation, and A/B testing of quiz entry points. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/publish-quiz/)
    
-   **Results height sync** – The results pages now automatically adjust their height based on displayed content, preventing layout jumps and improving the smoothness of transitions between sections.
    
-   **Quiz Copilot now generates email templates** – Quiz Copilot can automatically craft email follow-ups for your quiz results - ideal for Klaviyo, Shopify Email, Omnisend, and more. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/use-quiz-copilot/)
    
-   **Discount codes in results** – Merchants can now dynamically display discount codes on the results page, improving conversion rates for first-time buyers and retargeting campaigns. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/add-discount/)
    
-   **Better mobile picture choice layout** – Picture-based choices now stack and scale more cleanly on mobile devices, improving readability and reducing accidental taps. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/add-adjust-images/)
    

### **Why this matters**

These updates focus heavily on front-end accuracy, mobile UX, and personalization, giving merchants more control over both design and conversion tools.

### **Conclusion**

September rounded out several long-requested UI and customization enhancements - especially for advanced quiz setups.
