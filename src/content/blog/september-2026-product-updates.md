September was about putting you in control: a new setting lets you decide how long customer-entered personal data stays in your quiz responses, cart drawers now follow your theme's own settings across more Shopify themes, and Copilot can walk you through the new privacy options.

## Highlights

-   **Choose how long personal data is kept.** A new quiz setting permanently deletes the information customers type into your quiz after 14, 30, 60, or 90 days, or keeps it indefinitely if you prefer. It clears names, emails, phone numbers, free text, numbers, and dates, while choice answers, recommendations, and your quiz analytics stay intact, so your reporting keeps working on data that no longer identifies anyone. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone, so the setting is unlocked by our team: contact support and we'll switch it on for your shop. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/reference/quiz-builder/quiz-settings/)
-   **Cart drawers across Maestrooo themes.** On Impact, Prestige, Focal, and Warehouse, adding a product from quiz results opens the cart drawer in place, so shoppers keep their spot in the quiz, and the drawer contents match the quiz counter straight away. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/update-shopify-cart-drawer/)
-   **Cart drawers respect your theme settings.** On Horizon-family themes, the drawer now follows the setting you chose in your theme. If you'd rather it stayed closed, adding a recommendation or removing an item leaves it closed. Your theme stays in charge of how the cart behaves.
-   **Precise question navigation.** Quizzes now register exactly one advance per answer, even on a quick double tap, so shoppers see every question you wrote.
-   **Broader WooCommerce connection support.** WooCommerce stores using the newest SSL certificates now pass the connection check and connect normally.
-   **Copilot knows the privacy settings.** Ask Quiz Copilot about personal data deletion and it will walk you through your options. [Read more](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/how-to-guides/use-quiz-copilot/)

## What's coming

-   **A new version of the Built for Shopify app.** The next version of the [Built for Shopify](/revenuehunt-built-for-shopify/) app is on its way, with more options for designing your quiz and your results page exactly the way you want them.
-   **Agent-ready commerce.** We're working on making your quiz's personal-shopper logic available beyond your store, so the same tailored guidance can reach customers wherever they start their shopping, including the AI assistants they increasingly turn to. More on this soon.

## Conclusion

September's main addition is the data retention setting: you choose whether customer-entered details are deleted after 14, 30, 60, or 90 days, and your choice answers, recommendations, and analytics stay intact either way. Alongside it, cart drawers follow your theme's own settings on Maestrooo and Horizon themes, quizzes advance one question per answer, and WooCommerce stores on the newest SSL certificates connect normally.
