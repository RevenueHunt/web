Shipping can make or break an e-commerce business, and with Shopify’s extensive ecosystem, the right app can transform your store’s logistics from a chore into a strategic asset. Whether you’re a one-person shop or scaling globally, here are the top Shopify shipping apps you should consider in **2025**, based on recent expert analyses and real-world performance.

## What Makes a Shipping App Great in Shopify?

Before diving into the top picks, here are key features that define a standout Shopify shipping app:

-   **Seamless Integration & Automation** – From order imports to label printing, automation saves time and reduces manual errors.
-   **Multi-Carrier Support & International Reach** – Compare real-time rates across multiple carriers and manage global shipping with duties, taxes, and customs.
-   **Branded Tracking & Notifications** – Deliver a quality customer experience with customized tracking pages and updates via email or SMS.
-   **Scalable Pricing & Flexibility** – Apps should cater to both low-volume beginners and high-volume enterprises with flexible, tiered pricing.
-   **Rule-Based Shipping & Analytics** – Let apps handle logistics through advanced rules and insights to optimize costs.

## Top Apps for Shopify Shipping 

Based on expert roundups, user reviews, and feature sets, here are the standout options:

### PH MultiCarrier Shipping Label

![](/img/blog/shopify-shipping-apps/mcsl-shipping-2.jpg)

[PH MultiCarrier Shipping Label](https://apps.shopify.com/multi-carrier-shipping-label?) lets you connect multiple shipping carriers to Shopify, display real-time rates at checkout, and print shipping labels directly from your store. It supports domestic and international shipping with leading carriers like UPS, FedEx, USPS, Canada Post, and DHL Express, as well as Indian services like Blue Dart, Delhivery, XPRESSBEES, and many more.  The [Shopify app](https://www.pluginhive.com/shopify-multi-carrier-shipping-label-app/) automates rate calculation, label generation, order fulfillment, and tracking notifications, saving time and reducing shipping errors.

**Key Features**

-   Display live carrier-calculated rates from multiple carriers at checkout.
-   Print shipping labels, packing slips, and commercial invoices in one click or bulk.
-   Automatically select the cheapest shipping service based on your rules.
-   Integrate with both global and regional carriers for expanded shipping coverage.
-   Send live tracking updates to customers and manage shipments via a merchant dashboard.

**Pricing**

-   **Basic** – $9/month: 1 carrier, 100 labels, no carrier-calculated rates
-   **Starter** – $19/month: 2 carriers, 200 labels, 30,000 rate calculations
-   **Popular** – $29/month: All carriers, 1,000 labels, 50,000 rate calculations
-   **Premium** – $49/month: All carriers, 3,000 labels, 100,000 rate calculations  
    (14-day free trial available for all plans)

**Reviews & Ratings**

-   **Rating:** 4.9/5 (517 reviews)
-   Merchants praise its ease of use, carrier flexibility, and time-saving automation features. Bulk label printing, smooth Shopify integration, and responsive support via email, phone, and Zoom are frequently highlighted.

**Best For**Shopify merchants looking to automate and streamline multi-carrier domestic and international shipping with real-time rates, quick label printing, and reliable tracking notifications.

### PH Ship Rate & Track for FedEx

**![](/img/blog/shopify-shipping-apps/FED-RATE.png)**

[PH Ship Rate & Track for FedEx](https://apps.shopify.com/fedex-shipping?) is a FedEx-certified Shopify app that connects your FedEx account to your store, enabling real-time rates at checkout, [one-click label printing](https://www.pluginhive.com/create-fedex-shipping-label-online/), and automated order fulfillment. It supports FedEx Express, Ground, LTL Freight, Ground Economy, and Hold at Location services, ensuring flexible shipping options for your customers. With direct Shopify integration, it reduces manual work, prevents pricing errors, and sends tracking updates automatically, keeping customers informed and your operations smooth.

**Key Features**

-   Display real-time FedEx rates and delivery estimates at checkout.
-   Print unlimited FedEx labels, packing slips, and shipping documents in one click or bulk.
-   Supports FedEx One Rate flat-rate boxes and custom packaging automation.
-   Mark orders as fulfilled and sends live FedEx tracking notifications to customers.
-   Access special services like FedEx Hold at Location, pickups, insurance, and ETD.

**Pricing**

-   **Unlimited Plan** – $19/month: Unlimited FedEx label printing, real-time rates, One Rate support, packaging automation, and 24/5 dedicated support  
    (15-day free trial available)

**Reviews & Ratings**

-   **Rating:** 4.9/5 (538 reviews)
-   Merchants love its accurate FedEx rate display, seamless Shopify integration, and time-saving automation. Specialized shipping support (e.g., FedEx Dry Ice, LTL Freight) and responsive onboarding assistance are standout features, with many praising the intuitive workflow once set up.

**Best For**Shopify merchants shipping exclusively with FedEx who need accurate rates, easy label printing, and advanced FedEx service support for domestic and international orders.

### ShipStation

**![](/img/blog/shopify-shipping-apps/PastedGraphic-1.png)**ShipStation is a multi-channel shipping and fulfillment solution for Shopify and other ecommerce platforms. It streamlines order management, automates shipping workflows, and offers discounted rates across top carriers. With support for domestic and international shipments, ShipStation saves time on label printing, tracking, and order syncing. Its multi-carrier platform and automation features help merchants scale efficiently and manage fulfillment from a single interface.

**Important Features**

-   Sync orders from multiple sales channels, including Shopify, Amazon, eBay, and Walmart.
-   Access discounted rates with major carriers like USPS, UPS, FedEx, Canada Post, and DHL Express.
-   Automate shipping workflows, including bulk label printing and order splitting.
-   Generate branded labels, packing slips, and tracking notifications.
-   Inventory sync and advanced order filtering for efficient fulfillment.

**Pricing**

-   **Starter** – $9.99/month: 50 shipments, 1 user, access to 300+ integrations, email & community support (30-day free trial)
-   **Growth** – $29.99/month: 500 shipments, branded labels, live chat & email support (30-day free trial)
-   **Scale** – $99.99/month: 2,000 shipments, 3 users, product bundles, live chat, email & phone support (30-day free trial)
-   **High-Volume** – $399.99/month: 7,500+ shipments, 10 users, automated rate shopper, extended phone support (30-day free trial)

**Reviews & Ratings**

-   **Rating:** 4.0/5 (551 reviews)
-   Merchants appreciate its multi-channel integration, discounted rates, and time-saving automation. Features like order tagging, automatic price checking for best rates, and bulk shipping are highlighted. Some users note occasional technical issues, but overall support and platform reliability receive positive feedback.

**Best For**Merchants with multiple sales channels seek a centralized shipping platform with automation, discounted rates, and tools for both domestic and international order fulfillment.

### Shippo – Simplified Shipping

**![](/img/blog/shopify-shipping-apps/shopify-shippo.webp)**Shippo –  Simplified Shipping is a multi-carrier shipping solution for Shopify that syncs orders, offers discounted rates (up to 90% off) from top carriers, and streamlines fulfillment in one place. It supports USPS, UPS, DHL Express, and more, allowing you to compare rates, print labels, manage returns, and track shipments with ease. Designed for scalability, Shippo helps automate workflows as your business grows.

**Key Features**

-   Sync Shopify orders in real time and connect multiple sales channels.
-   Access discounted carrier rates or connect your own accounts.
-   Batch print shipping labels and packing slips for multiple orders.
-   Create branded tracking pages, notifications, and return labels.
-   Track shipments in real time with automated customer updates.

**Pricing**

-   **Starter** – Free: Up to 30 labels/month, discounted rates, tracking, returns, automations, email support, 1 user
-   **Professional** – From $19/month: Up to 10K labels/month, connect carrier accounts, branded shipping, chat & phone support, up to 5 users  
    (Free 30-day trial available)

**Reviews & Ratings**

-   **Rating:** 3.7/5 (265 reviews)
-   Merchants appreciate the discounted rates and convenience for small to medium-scale shipping, but mention mixed experiences with customer service and insurance claims. Some long-term users noted changes in pricing structure as a drawback.

**Best For**Merchants are looking for a cost-effective, entry-level multi-carrier shipping platform with built-in rate discounts and basic automation features.

### ShippingEasy

**![](/img/blog/shopify-shipping-apps/PastedGraphic-2.png)**ShippingEasy is a shipping and order management solution that helps Shopify merchants simplify fulfillment, access discounted USPS and UPS rates, and automate shipping tasks. Designed to save time and reduce costs, the platform includes automation rules, presets, and real-time support.

-   **Plus** – $49.99/month: 1,500 shipments/month, discounted rates, Flat Rate Green pricing, unlimited users (30-day free trial)

**Reviews & Ratings**

-   **Rating:** 4.3/5 (351 reviews)
-   Merchants appreciate the automation, rate savings, and multi-channel integration. Positive feedback highlights time savings and responsive customer support, though some users note occasional technical issues and login changes.

**Best For**Merchants seeking discounted USPS/UPS rates, shipping automation, and built-in marketing tools for multi-channel ecommerce fulfillment.

## **Closing Thoughts**

Each of these apps tackles shipping challenges differently, from automation and cost-saving to customer experience and international logistics. Start with the one that matches your current volume and regional needs. You can always scale or switch later as your business grows.
