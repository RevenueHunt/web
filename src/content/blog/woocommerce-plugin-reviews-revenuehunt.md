## Two install paths, same engine

You can install the RevenueHunt quiz on WooCommerce two ways: from the **[WordPress.org plugin directory](https://wordpress.org/plugins/product-recommendation-quiz-for-ecommerce/)** or from the **[WooCommerce.com extension marketplace](https://woocommerce.com/products/product-recommendation-quiz-for-woocommerce/)**. Both are free, both run the same underlying engine, and both put you on the Free plan (100 quiz responses per month with full feature access). Pick whichever store you're more comfortable installing from.

## Who installs the plugin

The 100+ reviewers span the categories where guided selling produces the most lift on WooCommerce:

- **Stores with wide product catalogues** that produce decision fatigue at the collection-page level. The quiz narrows the selection to a confident recommendation.
- **Seasonal and promotional campaigns** that need a different recommendation flow each quarter without rebuilding the storefront.
- **Subscription box services** that need to match customers with the right starter box.
- **Health and wellness, skincare, supplements** where pre-purchase scepticism is highest and a consultation-style quiz earns trust.
- **Customer-retention-focused stores** that want to keep existing customers engaged via personalised re-engagement quizzes.

## What's behind the 5.0 rating

A perfect 5.0 across 100 reviews isn't a vanity number; it's a structural consequence of three things the plugin does that most WooCommerce quiz tools don't:

1. **Generous free tier.** 100 quiz responses per month with full feature access (Klaviyo integration, custom CSS, conditional logic, full Quiz Builder). Wrong-fit merchants self-select out before they leave a poor review; right-fit merchants have enough room to validate the model before paying.
2. **Same-day support response.** Merchants name Alex, Paulina and Erik specifically in their reviews. Same-day response (often within minutes) is the norm, including across European, North American and APAC time zones.
3. **Active maintenance.** The plugin is updated regularly (version 2.3.10 at the time of writing, last updated within the past week), tested up to the latest WordPress 7.0, and ships in 15 languages.

## Frequently asked questions

### What's the current rating on the WordPress.org plugin directory?

5.0 stars across 100 verified reviews, with **100% of reviews rating five stars** (zero 4/3/2/1-star reviews at the time of writing). The plugin is installed on 2,000+ active WooCommerce stores.

### Should I install from WordPress.org or WooCommerce.com?

Either works. Both are free, both run the same engine, both put you on the Free plan (100 quiz responses per month with every feature unlocked). Pick the marketplace you're more comfortable installing from. If you outgrow the Free plan, the upgrade path is identical from either source.

### Does the WooCommerce plugin work with my theme?

Yes for any standard WooCommerce theme. The plugin embeds via a shortcode that you can drop into any page or post, or as a popup that fires across the site. The quiz inherits typography and basic styles from the theme; deeper visual customisation is available via the Quiz Builder's design panel and full custom CSS on higher tiers.

### What integrations does the WooCommerce plugin support?

Klaviyo, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Google Analytics 4, Facebook Pixel, and hundreds more via Zapier. For the most common pairing (Klaviyo segmentation), see [how Klaviyo segmentation unlocks once zero-party data lands in profiles](https://revenuehunt.com/klaviyo-zero-party-data/). For the Mailchimp-specific setup, see [how to use Mailchimp for post-quiz email marketing](https://revenuehunt.com/how-to-use-mailchimp-for-post-quiz-email-marketing/).

### What makes the support stand out across so many reviews?

Three things merchants consistently name: response time in minutes (not days), real solutions rather than canned templates, and specific team members (Alex, Paulina, Erik) being named in reviews from multiple countries and time zones. Most plugin support teams don't get named at all.
