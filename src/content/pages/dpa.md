Last updated: September 10, 2026.

RevenueHunt products are operated by two independent companies, and each one publishes its own Data Processing Agreement. Use the DPA for the platform your store runs on.

| Your platform | Operating entity | Data Processing Agreement |
|---------------|------------------|---------------------------|
| Shopify | Durian Capital Inc., British Virgin Islands (company number 2109760) | [Shopify DPA](/dpa-shopify/) |
| WordPress and WooCommerce | Dairy Capital Limited, England and Wales (company number 12503996) | [WooCommerce DPA](/dpa-woocommerce/) |

Both DPAs form part of the [Terms of Service](/tos/) and bind the Parties in electronic form, as permitted by Article 28(9) GDPR. Each one includes the sub-processor list, the technical and organizational security measures, and the transfer mechanisms that apply to that entity.

For questions about either DPA, contact [info@revenuehunt.com](mailto:info@revenuehunt.com).
