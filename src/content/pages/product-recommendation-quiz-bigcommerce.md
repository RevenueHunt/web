**HOW TO GET STARTED**

-   Navigate to our [BigCommerce Marketplace listing](https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/product-recommendation-quiz/) and click on “get this app”
-   grant permissions to connect our app to your BigCommerce store
-   In your BigCommerce dashboard, go to “Apps” and click on our app
-   Follow the **Success Checklist** to create and publish your quiz
-   Drive traffic to your quiz and start getting sales and leads!

Check out our [documentation site](https://docs.revenuehunt.com/) for more information on how to create and publish your first Product Recommendation Quiz.
