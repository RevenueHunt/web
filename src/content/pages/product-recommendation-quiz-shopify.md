### Convert casual shoppers into confident buyers with a product recommendation quiz.

Build beautiful, high-converting product recommendation quizzes - no coding needed. RevenueHunt helps you guide every shopper to their perfect product, collect valuable data, and drive measurable results across your store and marketing channels.

Create quizzes with our AI-powered quiz builder, gain valuable insights, and sync with your mailing list or CRM to create high-ROI marketing campaigns. Start creating personalized shopping experiences that boost engagement, conversions, and loyalty.

-   **Boost sales** – Guide customers to the right products with quizzes that convert.
-   **Collect actionable data** – Segment and tag your shoppers based on their responses.
-   **Send captured leads to your CRM** or mailing list to run personalized campaigns.
-   **Match your brand** – Customize images, fonts, layout, and CSS to fit your style.
-   **Premium support** – Work with one of our account managers to build and launch your quiz.

### How to get started

-   Navigate to our [Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/product-recommendation-quiz-revenuehunt?utm_source=revenuehunt.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=product-recommendation-quiz-shopify) and click on "add app"
-   Grant permissions to connect our app to your Shopify store
-   In your Shopify dashboard, go to "Apps" and click on our app
-   Follow the **Success Checklist** to create and publish your quiz
-   Drive traffic to your quiz and start getting sales and leads!

### Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I build a product quiz in Shopify?</strong></summary>

Install the app, pick a template, add your questions, and link choices to products or collections. Publish as an inline page, popup, or embed. No code required - most merchants launch in under 15 minutes.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is there a free quiz builder for Shopify?</strong></summary>

Yes - start on our free plan. You can build and publish a fully working quiz with core features enabled. When you outgrow monthly response limits, upgrade without losing data.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What's the best quiz funnel to increase AOV?</strong></summary>

Keep 5–8 high-intent questions, collect email before results (optional), then show 3–5 tailored products with badges, bundles, and quantity or variant selectors. Add cross-sells on the results page and pass UTM for clean attribution.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it integrate with Klaviyo, GA4, and Meta Pixel?</strong></summary>

Yes - send quiz answers and recommended products to Klaviyo profiles or lists for segmented flows. GA4 and Meta Pixel both receive events like quiz started, question viewed, and results viewed, so you can attribute revenue, build audiences, and optimize campaigns.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What's the best skincare quiz for Shopify?</strong></summary>

Use our skincare template: capture skin type, concerns, sensitivity or ingredients, and routine goals. Map answers to cleansers, serums, and moisturizers, then show a routine with usage tips and add-to-cart - great for LTV and fewer returns.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I build a size quiz?</strong></summary>

Absolutely. Ask fit preferences and key measurements, match answers to product variants or size charts, and output a recommended size per item. Show fit notes and a "why this size" explanation to boost confidence and reduce exchanges.
</details>
