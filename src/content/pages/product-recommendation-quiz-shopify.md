### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How do I build a product quiz in Shopify?

Install the app, pick a template, add your questions, and link choices to products or collections. Publish as an inline page, popup, or embed. No code required—most merchants launch in under 15 minutes.

#### Is there a free quiz builder for Shopify?

Yes—start on our free plan. You can build and publish a fully working quiz with core features enabled. When you outgrow monthly response limits, upgrade without losing data.

#### What’s the best quiz funnel to increase AOV?

Keep 5–8 high-intent questions, collect email before results (optional), then show 3–5 tailored products with badges, bundles, and quantity or variant selectors. Add cross-sells on the results page and pass UTM for clean attribution.

#### Does it integrate with Klaviyo, GA4, and Meta Pixel?

Yes—send quiz answers and recommended products to Klaviyo profiles or lists for segmented flows. GA4 and Meta Pixel both receive events like quiz started, question viewed, and results viewed, so you can attribute revenue, build audiences, and optimize campaigns.

#### What’s the best skincare quiz for Shopify?

Use our skincare template: capture skin type, concerns, sensitivity or ingredients, and routine goals. Map answers to cleansers, serums, and moisturizers, then show a routine with usage tips and add-to-cart—great for LTV and fewer returns.

#### Can I build a size quiz?

Absolutely. Ask fit preferences and key measurements, match answers to product variants or size charts, and output a recommended size per item. Show fit notes and a “why this size” explanation to boost confidence and reduce exchanges.
