### Convert casual shoppers into confident buyers with a product recommendation quiz built for Wix Stores.

RevenueHunt is a no-code product recommendation quiz that drops into any Wix Stores site. Guide every shopper to the right product, collect zero-party data your marketing can actually use, and link results straight back to your Wix product pages — without touching Wix's editor more than is necessary.

Most Wix merchants ship their first quiz in under 30 minutes. The quiz lives on a quiz-builder-hosted page (or embeds as an iframe inside your Wix site), and recommendations link directly to Wix product detail pages so cart and checkout stay native.

-   **Boost sales** – Guide shoppers to the right product with personalized recommendations that convert better than generic category pages.
-   **Collect actionable data** – Segment and tag every quiz-taker so you can run targeted email and ad campaigns afterwards.
-   **Sync your Wix catalog** – Connect via your Wix product feed (CSV or Google Shopping format) so prices and stock stay accurate.
-   **Match your brand** – Customize fonts, colors, layout, and CSS so the quiz looks like a native section of your Wix site.
-   **Premium support** – Work with one of our account managers to plan and launch your first quiz.

### How Wix product recommendation quizzes work

A shopper lands on your Wix site, opens the quiz (inline section, popup, or dedicated landing page), and answers 4 to 8 short questions about their needs, preferences, or constraints. Conditional logic skips irrelevant questions automatically — a beginner never gets asked about advanced specs, a gift-buyer never gets asked about themselves.

When the quiz ends, RevenueHunt scores each answer against your Wix product catalog and returns the 2 to 5 best matches with a personalized explanation per product. Shoppers click through to your Wix product detail pages, where they add to cart natively. Every event (quiz started, question answered, result viewed, click-through) fires to GA4, Meta Pixel, and your email platform so you can attribute revenue cleanly.

The whole experience is hosted outside Wix's editor, which means it doesn't compete with Wix's velocity caps, doesn't slow down your site, and doesn't break when Wix updates its template engine.

### What's different about quizzes on Wix

Wix is the most accessible storefront platform for small businesses, but its app ecosystem is smaller than Shopify's and its scripting surface is more limited. The quiz integration sidesteps those constraints by living outside the Wix editor and reaching back into your catalog through a feed. The trade-off: the quiz isn't installed as a Wix App, so you won't find us in the Wix App Market. That's a feature, not a bug — it means you get the same quiz engine that powers Shopify and WooCommerce merchants, including AI-assisted question generation, A/B testing, and the full analytics stack.

For Wix Studio sites (the newer designer-focused product), the embed flexibility is even higher — you can drop the quiz as a custom widget anywhere in the layout.

### When is a Wix product recommendation quiz useful?

Wix stores typically have smaller catalogs than Shopify or BigCommerce stores, but the same buyer-confusion problem applies. A quiz shines when:

-   Your catalog has 20+ SKUs and shoppers struggle to choose
-   Your products have variations (size, scent, strength, style) that confuse first-time buyers
-   You want a higher-converting alternative to the "shop all" page
-   You want to collect email addresses without a generic newsletter popup
-   You're running paid traffic and need a conversion-focused landing page

Top verticals that see the strongest results on Wix:

-   Beauty, skincare, and cosmetics
-   Supplements and wellness
-   Apparel and accessories
-   Specialty foods and beverages
-   Handmade and DTC craft brands

### Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I build a product recommendation quiz on Wix Stores?</strong></summary>

Sign up for a RevenueHunt account, connect your Wix product feed, pick a template, write your questions, and link answers to products. Embed the quiz as an iframe section in your Wix editor, or link out to the hosted quiz page. No code required.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is RevenueHunt available in the Wix App Market?</strong></summary>

No — the quiz lives outside the Wix editor and connects to your store via a product feed. This means you get the full RevenueHunt feature set (including AI question generation, A/B testing, and advanced analytics) that wouldn't fit inside Wix's app sandbox.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is there a free product recommendation quiz for Wix?</strong></summary>

Yes — start on our free plan with 100 quiz responses per month and the full builder unlocked. Upgrade when you outgrow the response limit; quiz structure and shopper data persist across tiers.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How does the quiz handle Wix product variants?</strong></summary>

If your Wix feed exports variants (size, color, scent), the quiz can recommend specific variants based on answers. Otherwise it recommends the parent product and lets shoppers pick a variant on the Wix product detail page.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it work with Wix Studio sites?</strong></summary>

Yes — Wix Studio's custom-widget support makes embedding even more flexible than classic Wix Editor. Drop the quiz iframe anywhere in your Studio layout, and it inherits the parent page's responsive behavior.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it integrate with Klaviyo, GA4, and Meta Pixel?</strong></summary>

Yes — quiz answers and recommended products flow to Klaviyo profiles or lists. GA4 and Meta Pixel both receive named events (quiz_started, question_viewed, result_viewed, outbound_to_product), so you can attribute revenue and build remarketing audiences.
</details>
