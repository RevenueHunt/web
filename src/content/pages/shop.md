# Boost sales on Shop with a quiz

Add a Product Recommendation Quiz to your [Shop](https://www.shopify.com/shop) store and start driving conversions and sales today.

[install on Shopify](https://apps.shopify.com/product-recommendation-quiz-revenuehunt/install)

## A salesperson for your Shop store

With RevenueHunt's Product Recommendation Quiz app, you can easily boost Shop sales by guiding your customers throughout their shopping experience and helping them find the right products.

![Recommend the Right Products](/img/pages/shop/recommend-right-products.svg)

### Recommend the Right Products

Shoppable quizzes drive sales, advise customers on finding the right products for them and help them make confident purchasing decisions.

![Know Your Customers](/img/pages/shop/know-your-customers.svg)

### Know Your Customers

Quizzes let you collect actionable data by asking your customers targeted questions. Segment and tag your shoppers based on their responses.

![Grow Your Audience](/img/pages/shop/grow-your-audience.svg)

### Grow Your Audience

Send captured leads to your mailing list or CRM. Leverage customer profile data to create more personalized, higher ROI marketing campaigns.

## Get started with Shop Quiz

Shop is an app which connects Shopify merchants with millions of engaged shoppers. Once eligible, merchants are automatically opted into selling on Shop. You can review [eligibility requirements here](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/eligibility).

To enable Shop as a new channel for your Shopify store and to grow your presence on the app, follow the steps in [this article](https://www.shopify.com/ca/blog/mobile-sales-shop). You can manage how you show up in the Shop app via the [Shop channel](https://www.shopify.com/shop).
