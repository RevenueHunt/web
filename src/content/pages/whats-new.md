Stay up to date with the latest improvements to RevenueHunt, from new features in our quiz builder to integrations, performance upgrades, and quality-of-life fixes.

This is the central place where we share what’s shipped, what’s changed, and what’s coming next — so you can get more value out of your quizzes without digging through release notes or emails.

### What you’ll find here

-   **New features** – big releases that change how you build, analyze, or embed quizzes.
-   **Improvements** – UX tweaks, performance upgrades, and workflow optimizations.
-   **Integrations** – updates to Shopify, Klaviyo, Meta, Google, and other tools you rely on.
-   **Fixes** – bug fixes and behind-the-scenes work that make everything run smoother.

If you’d like to get these updates by email as well, keep an eye on [our monthly newsletter](#quiz-xVHLQz) — we regularly highlight the most important changes and how to use them in your store.
